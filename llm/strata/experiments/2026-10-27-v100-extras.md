# 2026-10-27 — V100 extras: quality, sustained output, warm cache, power, kernels

Follow-up to the 2026-10-26 V100 residency campaign
([`2026-10-26-v100-residency-kernels.md`](2026-10-26-v100-residency-kernels.md)).
One allocation on **volta05** (4× Tesla V100-SXM2-32GB, sm_70, 380 GB RAM),
Strata 0.1.50 **cuda12**, sequential. The 2026-10-26 numbers are the baseline;
nothing there was re-run.

## H1 — quality at long context

Needle: *"The access code for cabinet BX-7741 is KITE-9Q7M."* embedded at
**10 / 50 / 90 %** of the filler characters; question at the end; **2 cold
repeats** (fresh salt each) per position; `max_tokens=512` (a thinking model
needs room to finish its reasoning before the answer). Tool canary per context:
a long filler followed by *"Use the read_file tool to read /etc/hostname …
You must call the tool"*, with an OpenAI `tools` list and
`tool_choice="required"`.

| pack | ctx | needle | tool | needle TTFT | prompt tokens |
|:--|---:|:--|:--|---:|---:|
| IQ3_S (4×) | 65,536 | **6/6** | **1/1** | 17.9 s | 64,633 |
| IQ3_S (4×) | 131,072 | **6/6** | **1/1** | 33.8 s | 130,260 |
| IQ3_S (4×) | 262,144 | **6/6** | **1/1** | 71.3 s | 261,484 |
| Coder IQ1_M (2×) | 65,536 | **6/6** | **1/1** | 23.3 s | 64,539 |
| Coder IQ1_M (2×) | 131,072 | **6/6** | **1/1** | 51.6 s | 130,044 |
| Q2_0 (2×) | 65,536 | **6/6** | **1/1** | 26.6 s | 64,539 |
| Q2_0 (2×) | 131,072 | **6/6** | **1/1** | 54.4 s | 130,044 |

**Needle 30/30, tool 7/7.** Every correct answer is exactly `KITE-9Q7M`
(no reasoning leakage); the tool canary returns
`tool_calls[0].function.name = read_file` with
`arguments = {"path":"/etc/hostname"}`, `tool_choice=required` accepted,
`finish_reason=tool_calls`. The three packs and all three context depths pass
at ~261.5k real prompt tokens (0.25 % below the 262,144 window).

Methodology note: the first NIAH attempt used a natural-language repeated
filler and `max_tokens=32`; it tokenized at ~5.8 chars/token (half-size
prompts) and the thinking model never reached an answer. Both are fixed:
the probe now uses `strata_probe.build_filler` (the filler the calibration
measures) and 512 tokens. The failed attempt is kept in
`_attempts/run-38245734/` and is **not** quoted as a quality result.

## H2 — sustained output at 262k

`probe.max_tokens=1024`, C1, 3 requests, same long-context server.

| ctx | real prompt / req | completion tokens | strict decode | TPOT | TTFT | aggregate |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 65,536 | 65,518 | 2,044 (3 req) | **87.1 tok/s** | **11.5 ms** | 17.3 s | 26.3 tok/s |
| 262,144 | 261,005 | 2,007 (3 req) | **72.5 tok/s** | **13.8 ms** | 70.4 s | 8.3 tok/s |

The model stopped before `max_tokens` on every request (about 670 tokens per
request, `finish_reason=stop`), so the strict decode window is 7.8 s (64k) and
9.2 s (262k), not the nominal ~14 s. The decode/TPOT numbers are over the
actual generation window (engine `predicted_ms`); aggregate includes the 70 s
prefill at 262k, which is why it is 8.3 tok/s despite 72.5 tok/s decode.

## H4 — 2× IQ3_S: the crossover

Job 38246490, `--gpus 0,1`, window 131,072, matrix `--batch 2`.

**Residency: 99 %, not 100 %.** The engine caches 12,288 pairs on CUDA0 and
**12,135 of 12,288 on CUDA1** (153 pairs, 0.6 %, miss), with
`99% of the experts resident` and only **370 MiB free on CUDA1** (the engine's
LOW warning; CUDA0 has 4,220 MiB free). 64 GB is *almost* enough for IQ3_S;
the 4× configuration is 100 % resident with 8.5–11.8 GiB free.

**Verdict.** 2× holds 99 % of IQ3_S and is a legitimate single-stream
short/medium-context configuration (C1 decode within 0–8 % of 4× up to 64k).
It is **not** a substitute for 4× at long context or C2: prefill is 1.5–5.7×
slower (TTFT 82 s vs 18 s at 64k C2; 192 s vs 34 s at 131k C2), aggregate is
1.3–4.6× lower, and CUDA1 is at 370 MiB free. Per-stream decode at C2 is
higher on 2× at 16k/64k/131k (fewer pipeline stages, batch 2), but that is a
latency-per-stream curiosity, not throughput.

## H5 — checkpoint chain on 4× vs the L40

`strata-chain-v100-4x`: 1k → 100k → 200k → 262144, 512 tokens/turn, +1 repeat
at the top.

| turn | stop | V100 reused | V100 f | V100 TTFT | V100 dec | L40 reused | L40 TTFT | L40 dec |
|--:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 1,024 | 0 | 0.00 | 1.48 s | 90.8 | 0 | 0.72 s | 129.3 |
| 2 | 100,000 | 0 | 0.00 | 24.80 s | 76.5 | 0 | 23.37 s | 122.2 |
| 3 | 200,000 | 98,304 | 0.49 | 33.38 s | 74.4 | 98,304 | 25.87 s | 117.8 |
| 4 | 262,144 | 196,608 | 0.75 | 29.36 s | 74.6 | 196,608 | 17.71 s | 116.6 |
| 4 (repeat) | 262,144 | 261,671 | 1.00 | **3.25 s** | 72.5 | 261,050 | **0.41 s** | 115.0 |

The checkpoint granularity is **identical** on V100 and L40: the 100k turn
reuses nothing (the 1k prompt is below the first 16,384-token checkpoint),
the 200k turn reuses 6 × 16,384 = 98,304, the 262k turn 12 × 16,384 =
196,608, and the byte-identical repeat reuses the whole prompt. The V100
chain pays 1.2–1.7× the L40 TTFT at the growth turns and 8× on the repeat
(3.25 s vs 0.41 s), with 0.63× the decode rate. The first growth turn (1k →
100k) is a full prefill on both machines; the chain only pays off from 100k
onward.

## M10 — NVLink / P2P

- All six GPU pairs are **NV2**: two bonded NVLinks, six 25.781 GB/s links
  per GPU (≈150 GB/s per GPU aggregate).
- `nvidia-smi topo -m` shows GPU0/1 on NUMA node 0 (CPUs 0-15,32-47) and
  GPU2/3 on NUMA node 1.
- No `p2pBandwidthLatencyTest` binary exists on the node, so no measured P2P
  bandwidth. The engine's layer split prints a **12.4 GB/s host→device PCIe**
  probe (used for the expert-miss cost), not an NVLink number; it says "one
  hand-off per window" but does not log which transport the stage hand-off
  uses. Hardware is NVLink-capable; the engine's PCIe figure is not evidence
  against that.

## M12 — cold start / load time

| phase | Lustre staging | launch → ready (node /tmp) |
|:--|---:|---:|
| IQ3_S 4× (long-context server) | 49 s (Lustre cache warm) | 96 s |
| Coder 2× | 718 s (~41 MB/s) | 47 s |
| Q2_0 2× | 107 s (cached) | 121 s |
| IQ3_S 4× (M11) | — (already staged) | _fill_ |

Cold staging from an evicted Lustre cache was 30–90 min per pack in the
2026-10-26 campaign (the 28.8 GB PLE shard is single-striped on one OST);
the 49 s figure here is with the shard already in the client cache. Engine
load from node-local `/tmp` is 47–121 s, so **launch→ready is ~1–2 min once
the pack is local**, and staging dominates deployment time.

## Findings

1. **Quality is not the problem at 262k.** Needle 30/30 and tool calls 7/7
   across IQ3_S (4×), Coder and Q2_0 at their tested contexts; 262k IQ3_S is
   6/6 at 261.5k real tokens. The earlier 0/18 was a probe bug (32-token
   thinking budget + non-canonical filler), not a model failure.
2. **Sustained output**: 72.5 tok/s / 13.8 ms TPOT at 262k and 87.1 tok/s /
   11.5 ms at 64k; the model stops at ~670 tokens, so 1024-token cells do
   not produce a 14 s window. Aggregate is prefill-bound (8.3 tok/s at 262k).
3. **2× IQ3_S is 99 % resident**, not fully: 153 of 24,576 pairs stream and
   CUDA1 has 370 MiB free. It is a valid single-stream short/medium config,
   not a 4× substitute at long context or C2 (TTFT 1.5–5.7× worse, aggregate
   1.3–4.6× lower).
4. **The chain is checkpoint-limited, not card-limited**: V100 and L40 reuse
   exactly the same 98,304 / 196,608 tokens; V100 pays 1.2–1.7× the TTFT and
   8× on the full-prefix repeat.
5. **Cost**: ~€5.81 per 1M generated tokens including prefill at 262k, or
   ~€0.65/1M decode-only (€0.52 idle-subtracted) at €0.20/kWh.
6. **NVLink is present (NV2, 6×25.781 GB/s/GPU)** but the engine does not log
   its stage transport; its 12.4 GB/s figure is the PCIe host probe.

## Caveats

- One run per cell; 2 cold NIAH repeats per position; tool canary once per
  context. The 262k NIAH TTFT is 71 s (prefill-dominated).
- H1's `correct_any` column is only populated for needle rows; tool rows use
  `correct` (1/1 each). The tool call is graded on the function name and
  arguments, not prose.
- H2's model stopped early (~670 of 1024 tokens); do not read the 1024-token
  cells as a 14 s sustained window.
- H4's 2× server uses `--batch 2`; the 4× baseline uses `--batch 4`. The
  C2 per-stream decode difference is partly that server shape.
- The H3 first run's nominal contexts were 43k/86k/173k (filler compression);
  only the corrected run is used for the context-specific claims.
- Power is GPU-only (`power.draw` sum), not wall power; the node's CPUs,
  fans and PSU losses are excluded. €0.20/kWh is an assumption.
- No P2P bandwidth binary on the node; no measured NVLink throughput.
- Quality is one needle and one tool shape per context, not a broad suite.

## Gap list

- Quality with many needles / distractors / multi-hop, and at 262k for Coder
  and Q2_0 (only 64k/131k measured).
- 1024-token sustained output with a prompt that actually forces 1024 tokens;
  the current cells stop at ~670.
- H3 at C>1 concurrent conversations (interleaved sequentially here) and more
  than 4 conversations (the cache is 8 GiB / 4 slots).
- 2× IQ3_S at 262k (not attempted; 64 GB is already at 370 MiB free at 131k).
- P2P/NVLink bandwidth on the node; whether the layer split's stage hand-off
  uses NVLink.
- M7/M8/M9/M11 conclusions (pending the recovery job).
- Wall power, PSU efficiency, and a longer stability run (>2 h).
