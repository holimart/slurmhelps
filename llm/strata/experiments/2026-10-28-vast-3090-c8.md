# 2026-10-28 — RTX 3090 on Vast.ai: C1–C8 throughput, the C16 engine cap, and knobs

The third consumer card after Titan RTX. Goal: measure batch scaling and
output tok/s for Qwen3.8-Flash-Next IQ3_S on one 24 GB RTX 3090, because the
economics table assumed a **C16 2.88×** multiplier that no 3090 run had ever
produced.

## The engine cap: C16 is not measurable in Strata

`strata launch --parallel 16` is rejected outright:

```text
strata launch: error: argument --parallel: 16 requests at once is outside what
the engine can do. Use 1 to 8.
```

The engine ceiling is **8 slots**. A 16-client test against 8 slots would be
oversubscription (C8 throughput with queueing), **not** a C16 batch gain; it is
not reported as one. The 2.88× C16 row stays a projection; a real C16 needs a
different engine (e.g. llama.cpp `-np 16`) or an engine change.

## Measured — IQ3_S, one RTX 3090

Aggregate tok/s (C1 / C2 / C4 / C8), per-stream C1 and C8, TTFT p50:

| ctx | C1 | C2 | C4 | C8 | C8/C1 | per-stream C1→C8 | TTFT p50 |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 4,096 | 41.9 | 39.5 | 48.1 | **54.0** | **1.29×** | 59.4 → 7.5 | 3.7 s |
| 16,384 | 24.5 | 22.0 | 23.6 | **25.0** | **1.02×** | 59.3 → 3.6 | 12.3 s |
| 32,768¹ | 17.3 | 15.9 | 15.9 | **16.4** | **0.95×** | 59.5 → 2.9 | 21.2 s |

¹ prompt clamped to 30,208 tokens (`max_context 32768 − 512 − 2048`).

- The C8 gain is **1.29× at best** and **negative at 32K**; the 2.88× projection
  is not merely optimistic, it is unsupported.
- Per-stream at C8 is **7.5 / 3.6 / 2.9 tok/s** — below the 10 tok/s interactive
  floor. C8 is a throughput mode, not a latency mode.
- At 32K the engine itself warns that eight slots exceed what a 24 GB card holds
  (it fits ~3); TTFT p95 for C8/32K was **263 s**.

At €1,900 capex and €0.20/kWh, energy+capex is scaled from the 1.3× row
(`iq3s_batch_sensitivity.csv`). Even the *measured* 1.29× row is **4.6× worse

Strata IQ3_S; the earlier €0.370 “1.3×” estimate was also generous because it
assumed 156.8 tok/s, whereas the measured C1 is 41.9 at 4K.

## Knobs (4K, C1, 512-token answers)

| variant | flags | decode | aggregate | Δ decode |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| base | — | 55.0 | 39.6 | — |
| **pf_fused** | `STRATA_PF_FUSED=1` | **56.4** | **40.6** | **+2.6%** |
| bf16_tc | `STRATA_BF16_TC=1` | 54.0 | 38.7 | −1.8% |
| spec_min_p0 | `--set spec-min-p=0` | 50.2 | 36.9 | −8.7% |
| hc_q8 | `STRATA_HC_Q8=1` | 51.2 | 22.3² | −7% |

² the HC_Q8 probe was interrupted when the server was stopped for the next
variant; treat its aggregate as indicative only.

Only `STRATA_PF_FUSED=1` helps, and only ~2.6%. The L40's `spec-min-p=0` win
(+15%) does **not** transfer to the 3090 at 4K with 512-token answers.

## Compilation options (untested here)

- The ready-made **cuda13** engine already contains sm_86 kernels; a source
  build with `-DCMAKE_CUDA_ARCHITECTURES=86 -DCMAKE_BUILD_TYPE=Release` is the
  remaining compile lever. No PGO/fast-math build was tested (a separate
  30–40 min experiment), and `--use_fast_math` would need a quality check.
- Runtime switches worth keeping: `STRATA_PF_FUSED=1` (+2.6%). Do **not** use
  `spec-min-p=0` on this card at 4K; BF16_TC and HC_Q8 did not help.
- The hard ceiling remains the **8-slot engine cap**; no flag or build changes
  that. A C16 study needs llama.cpp (`-np 16`) or an engine change.

## Caveats
