# 2026-10-23 — Qwen3.5 smoke tests on 2× A100 (A100 only)

Two Qwen3.5 4-bit checkpoints were downloaded and served with **stock vLLM
0.32** on `ampere02` (2× A100 80 GB). No H100 was used.

## Checkpoints

| Model | HF repo | Quant | Params / active |
|---|---|---|---:|
| Qwen3.5-35B-A3B | `cyankiwi/Qwen3.5-35B-A3B-AWQ-4bit` | compressed-tensors 4-bit, WNA16/Marlin | 35B / 3B |
| Qwen3.5-122B-A10B | `cyankiwi/Qwen3.5-122B-A10B-AWQ-4bit` | compressed-tensors 4-bit, WNA16/Marlin | 122B / 10B |

Local paths:

```text
/work/ffa/holecem/models/qwen35-35b-a3b-awq
/work/ffa/holecem/models/qwen35-122b-a10b-awq
```

Both checkpoints declare native `max_position_embeddings=262144`; **700K is
not a supported context for these two checkpoints** unless a long-context
extension is validated separately.

## Serving shape

- vLLM 0.32.0, BF16 KV, prefix caching, chunked prefill, eager mode.
- 35B: **TP1, one A100**, port 8001.
- 122B: **TP2, two A100**, port 8002, `--disable-custom-all-reduce`.
- `max_model_len=262144`, `max_num_seqs=32`, `max_num_batched_tokens=8192`.
- Kernel selection: FlashAttention 2, Triton/FLA GDN prefill, Marlin WNA16
  MoE.

## Capacity

| Model | GPU KV | Max concurrency at 262K |
|---|---:|---:|
| 35B-A3B | **2,287,334 tokens** | **8.73×** |
| 122B-A10B | **2,561,861 tokens** | **9.77×** |

The 122B KV capacity is higher despite TP2 because its Mamba/GDN state and
head layout differ; both are far above the 32-sequence request cap.

## Load times

| Model | Load time | Notes |
|---|---:|---|
| 35B-A3B | **~8–10 min** | 23 GiB checkpoint, one GPU |
| 122B-A10B | **~26 min** | 75 GiB checkpoint, two GPUs, Lustre I/O |

The 122B load includes a long prefetch/read phase from Lustre; the 35B is much
cheaper and is the better smoke target.

## Operational fixes found

- Stock vLLM on the compute node needs `CC=gcc CXX=g++`; otherwise Triton's
  `CudaUtils` compile fails with `PermissionError: [Errno 13] ... ''`.
- The 122B TP2 run initially spun at 100% CPU in NCCL/custom all-reduce without
  using GPU memory; `--disable-custom-all-reduce` fixed it.
- The 23 GiB/75 GiB checkpoints are on Lustre. Load time is I/O-bound and
  varies with competing jobs; the 122B is the more expensive target.

## Verdict

Both pass the smoke matrix on A100 only.

- **Qwen3.5-35B-A3B** is the clear low-risk next target: one GPU, 23 GiB,
  ~8–10 min load, ~164 output tok/s at C=8 for 500-token contexts.
- **Qwen3.5-122B-A10B** is usable on two A100s and stronger, but costs ~26 min
  to load and ~110 tok/s at C=8.
- Neither is a 700K-context model at its declared 262K limit. For 700K, keep
  the GLM REAP50 768K path or evaluate a long-context extension/DeepSeek V4
  Flash on larger hardware.
